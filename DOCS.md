# Wiki-MasterBetter — Developer Documentation

This is a plain-language, developer-facing version of the feature overview found in the [`wiki-masterbetter.me.js`](./wiki-masterbetter.me.js)
source file (the big comment block near the top of the file, just under the Tampermonkey header). That block is the
project's source of truth — every entry below keeps the same `Rule N` / `Feature XYZ` label used there and in the file,
so you can search the source for that exact label to find the matching implementation and its own, more detailed
comment.

The list below is **not reordered** — it stays in the order features were originally introduced, matching the source
file's own rule.

Developers advice: this document is totally written by Claude, the AI assistant, and is not guaranteed to be correct.
If you find a mistake, please report it in the [GitHub issues](https://github.com/QuentindiMeo/wiki-masterbetter/issues).

## A map of the site's pages

A few page routes on [wiki-masters.com](https://www.wiki-masters.com) come up repeatedly below:

| Route                 | What it is                                                        |
| --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/marketplace`        | The marketplace home page, where cards are listed for auction     |
| `/marketplace/{id}`   | A single card's bidding ("auction") page                          |
| `/profile`            | Your own profile page                                             |
| `/profile/{username}` | Someone else's profile page                                       |
| `/pulls`              | The page where you open your card packs                           |
| `/collection`         | Your personal card collection                                     |
| `/global-collection`  | The "wishlist" view, where you can browse friends' wishlist cards |
| `/trades`             | Your trade history with other users                               |
| `/friends`            | Your friends list                                                 |

## Rules and Features

- **Rule 1a — Bigger notifications list.** Makes the notifications panel larger, so more notifications fit on screen at once without extra scrolling.
- **Rule 1b — Declutter auction pages.** On auction pages (`/marketplace/{UUID}`), hides the non-essential round buttons next to the card (wishlist and market analysis buttons). Applied once the page has finished rendering, and again when navigating to an auction page without a reload.
- **Rule 2 — Smarter "mark as read".** Clicking a notification also marks every notification older than it as read, not just the one you clicked.
- **Rule 3 — Descriptive tab title.** Adds useful context to the browser tab's title depending on what you're looking at — for example the card currently being bid on, or the username of the profile you're viewing.
- **Rule 4 — 3-column friends grid.** Rearranges your friends list into a tidy 3-column grid instead of one long single-column list.
- **Rule 5 — Compact friend action buttons.** Shrinks the "Message" and "Trade" buttons on the friends grid down to just their icon, to save space. The "Message" button of the trades shown in the "Envoyées" and "Historique" tabs of `/trades` is shortened the same way.
- **Rule 6 — Collapsible navigation bar.** The sidebar navigation is compact by default, showing only icons, and does not react to hover: it stays as it is until Feature NPN's pin button expands it (smoothly) or compacts it back. In the compact bar the three buttons at the bottom of the bar (settings, tutorials, pin), docked to its very end whatever the height of the window are stacked, the logo gives way to the "✓ WMB" mark and the notification bell sits under it. Hovering (or focusing) a link of the compact bar shows its label in a small styled tooltip floating to the right of the bar, with an arrow pointing at the link (not shown once the bar is expanded, where the labels are visible).
- **Feature NPN — Pin the navigation bar.** Adds a pin button that switches the sidebar between its compact display (default) and its expanded one, both static. The choice is remembered between visits. (This feature replaced the original Rule 8 — see below.)
- **Rule 7 — Click the first notification to clear all.** Clicking the very top (most recent) notification marks the entire notifications list as read.
- _Rule 8 no longer exists as its own item — it was folded into Feature NPN (the pinnable navigation bar) above._
- **Rule 9 — Auto-open "My Bids".** Arriving on the marketplace automatically switches you to the tab chosen in the Config tab ("Onglet du marché ouvert par défaut": "Parcourir", "Mes enchères" by default, "Historique", "Gagnées", or "Aucun" to leave the site's own default). Only the chosen tab is ever clicked, once per arrival; changing the setting never clicks anything on a page that is already open. On the "Parcourir" tab, once you have typed or pasted something in the search field and paused for a moment (0.4 s, so the page never re-renders in the middle of a paste), the sort selector is switched from "Récemment listées" to "Fin imminente" (once each time you reach the tab, so changing it yourself afterwards sticks).
- **Feature TBC — Quick action button on collection cards.** Adds a small button directly on each card in your collection that opens its sale/estimate modal in one click, instead of needing to open the card's modal yourself first. Feature EBC's bulk evaluation tools click through this same button automatically.
- **Rule 10 — Live notification counter.** Clicking a notification immediately updates the unread-count badge to match, instead of waiting for a refresh.
- **Rule 11 — Dim the "buy packs" banner.** Once you have zero packs left to open, the site's promotional banner encouraging you to get more is faded down instead of staying at full visibility.
- **Rule 12 — Clearer profile action buttons.** Improves the visibility/contrast of the action buttons shown on user profile pages, which all turn a muted green when hovered instead of the site's red (the "Suivi" button of a followed player stays red). On your own profile page, also caps the width of the tag-count list so it doesn't stretch the full width of the page, and keeps the tag names from being clipped at the bottom (descenders of letters like Q, G or g).
- **Feature PNP — Pack pile-up notifications.** Sends a browser push notification when you're accumulating a lot of unopened card packs, as a reminder to go open them. It starts once the pile reaches a share of its maximum (10 packs) set in the "Seuil d'alerte de la pile de paquets" setting of Feature CFG (50 %, 70 %, 90 % by default, 100 %, i.e. from 5, 7, 9 or 10 packs), then repeats on every further increase; lowering below that share resets the tracking, so changing the setting does not replay old alerts.
- **Rule 13 — Trim long, fully-read notification lists.** Once every notification has been read, only the 20 most recent are shown at first; scrolling to the bottom loads the rest.
- **Feature TMB — Group bids by status.** Adds a toggle that splits your "My Bids" list into two groups: bids where someone has outbid you, and bids where you're still in the lead.
- **Feature CME — Close modals with Escape.** Lets you press the Escape key to close any open popup/modal, instead of having to find and click a close button.
- **Feature PNB — Bid-ending push alert.** Sends a browser push notification when one of your bids is about to end (30 seconds left), so you don't miss the chance to raise it.
- **Feature CAR — Claim all rewards at once.** Adds a "claim all" button on the achievements page that adds up and collects every pending reward in a single click, instead of one by one.
- **Feature AKP — Keyboard control on /pulls.** Lets you use the keyboard while opening packs: the arrow keys move between the pulled cards, Up/Down arrows jump straight to the first/last one, Space opens or closes a pack, E opens the currently selected card's full-size modal, and V does the same as E and then also puts that card up for sale, giving the focus to the price field so you can type the price right away. While a card is shown large, a small legend in the space to its left reminds you of the arrow shortcuts.
- **Feature SAT — Select all untagged cards.** Adds a "select all except tagged" button next to the site's own "select all" button on your collection page, so you can quickly select only the cards you haven't tagged yet.
- **Feature LBD — Enter key starts a bid.** While the card sell modal is open, pressing Enter with your cursor in any of its fields (the starting bid in particular) starts the auction immediately, without needing to click the "Lancer l'enchère" button. It works on any page where that modal can be opened, and does nothing in other modals. On a bid page (`/marketplace/{UUID}`), Enter also works in the bid field (it clicks "Miser") and, on one of your own auctions, in the lowered starting bid field (it clicks "Appliquer"); a disabled button is not clicked.
- **Rule 14 — Clearer wishlist auction notifications.** Rewrites the wording of notifications about auctions for cards on your wishlist so they read more clearly, wrapping the card's name in guillemets and tinting "mise en vente" pink.
- **Rule 15 — Relabel the back button.** On a card's bidding page, renames the back button to a clearer French label ("Retour en arrière") instead of the site's default wording.
- **Feature PLB — (Obsolete) Quick profile links on bid pages.** Used to add a small "@" link right after each bidder's name (and the seller's name) on a card's bidding page. No longer in use: its code has been removed, and its number is kept only so the numbering of the other rules/features stays stable.
- **Rule 16 — Auto-scroll to your last unread notification.** Opening the notifications panel scrolls it straight down to your most recent unread notification, instead of starting at the top of the list.
- **Feature BSP — Track and grade sale prices.** Remembers how much each card has previously sold for (stored in your browser, not on the server) — up to 7 prices per card by default (configurable in the Config tab, "Prix de vente gardés par carte": 5, 7, 10 or 15; lowering it deletes nothing by itself, a card's longer list is only trimmed, highest prices first, the next time it is recorded or imported), of which only the lowest ones (5 by default, configurable in the Config tab, "Prix retenus pour la moyenne de référence": 3, 5 or 7, never more than the stored cap) are used to compute the reference average — and uses that history to flag whether the current asking price looks like a good deal — or, on a sale you make yourself, how good that sale was. The top "legendary price" tier additionally requires the price to be at least 50 below the reference average. Unsold ("Non vendue") auctions are recorded too, except your own auctions, which are never recorded whatever their outcome (nor are cancelled ones). Every price it has recorded is listed in a table on your own profile page (the block, with its export/import and cache-clearing buttons, is there even before anything was recorded, so a fresh browser can import a file) (folded by default; a switch next to its title unfolds it; the export/import and cache-clearing buttons sit in their own row just above it, always visible), along with export/import buttons — export downloads a CSV file and import reads one back — so you can carry that history over to another browser or device (a small hint next to the export/import buttons reminds you that importing only adds, never deletes). The export also carries the list of players you follow (Feature FOL), merged into your current list on import instead of replacing it, and your personal player descriptions (Feature UDS), of which an imported one is only kept for a player you have no description for. Importing is strictly additive: nothing in your current cache is ever deleted. A card's estimate from the file is only used if you have none, or if yours is "?" and the file's is a real value (two different real values: yours stays); a card's recorded prices are combined with the file's (same auction counted once) and, if that exceeds the configured price limit, only the highest surplus prices are dropped, as when recording. Importing the same file twice changes nothing, and if it brings nothing new you are told so instead of the page reloading. The cache-clearing button asks for confirmation, then deletes every recorded sale price and every saved market estimate (Feature ETS); followed players and descriptions are kept. The size shown on it is the cache's estimated weight as that CSV file: its stored (JSON) size divided by 1.8, the measured JSON-to-CSV cost ratio.
- **Rule 17 — Reposition "mark all as read".** Moves the "mark all as read" notifications button to the left side of the notifications panel, instead of wherever the site puts it by default.
- **Rule 18 — Auto-focus useful inputs.** Opening a card's modal automatically puts your cursor in its filter field (on `/collection` and `/pulls`), which opens its dropdown; while that dropdown is open, Home and End select its first and last item. Arriving on `/collection` also automatically focuses the search field (Rule 18b). Each can be toggled separately in the settings.
- **Feature ETS — Remember market value estimates.** Saves each card's estimated market value (seen on `/pulls` and `/collection`) in your browser, then displays that saved value next to the card's rarity tag on your collection page, so you don't have to look it up again.
- **Feature FCP — Toggle individual features.** Adds a settings button, next to the navbar's pin button, that lets you turn each feature of this script on or off individually; your choices are saved and take effect the next time the page loads.
- **Rule 19 — Cleaner notification text.** Rewrites notification wording in one single, consistent pass rather than several separate small tweaks.
- **Feature EBC — Bulk-evaluate your collection.** Adds buttons on `/collection` to estimate the market value of every card at once (or only the ones still missing an estimate), with a stop button to cancel partway through, plus a button to sort the whole page by estimated value (most to least expensive first; each new click flips between descending and ascending, the button showing ▼ or ▲). These buttons are grouped in a dock at the far end of the rarity filter row (pushed to the right), and the site's own rarity filter buttons are kept ahead of that dock whenever the site re-adds them. A fourth, dark red "Estimer tout" button repeats the "missing estimate" pass across every page of your collection automatically, advancing the pager itself between pages; its label shows a minimum duration in minutes (one minute per page left, current page included, read from the site's "Page X / Y" indicator); the same stop button cancels it at any point. Keep the tab running the evaluation active and the browser window un-minimized for the whole run — background tabs and minimized windows get their timers throttled by the browser, which this feature's polling relies on, so a run left in the background is liable to stall or slow individual cards down. A card is never skipped for a slow market response: if its estimate hasn't arrived after a few seconds (8 s on the first attempt, 20 s on the retries by default; configurable in the Config tab as "Rapide" 4 s / 10 s, "Standard", or "Patient" 15 s / 40 s, read at the start of each run), its modal is closed and re-opened, up to the configured number of attempts, until the value is recorded (or you press the stop button). Cards marked as favorite are skipped by every bulk evaluation (they have no "Mettre aux enchères" button, so their estimate can't be reached) and aren't counted in the estimated durations. A card that can't be put up for sale either (no "Mettre aux enchères" button in its modal, e.g. already on auction or in a trade, or no card image to click) is skipped right away, and one that still has no estimate after a configurable number of attempts (Config tab, 1, 3 by default or 5) is skipped too, instead of looping on it forever; each skip is logged as a warning and the skipped cards are listed when the run ends. The quick action (Feature TBC) now looks for the sell button inside the card modal only, and waits a short while for it, so an unrelated button of the page can't be clicked instead.
- **Rule 20 — Tag list follows keyboard selection.** Inside a card's modal (on `/collection` and `/pulls`), the tag list automatically scrolls to keep whichever tag you've highlighted with the keyboard in view.
- **Feature GCP — Click a friend's name to see their profile.** On the global collection/wishlist page, clicking the username of a friend who owns a card takes you straight to that friend's profile.
- **Rule 21 — Hide the legal disclaimer in the card modal.** Removes the small legal-notice text from a card's modal (on `/collection` and `/pulls`) to leave more room for the actual card details.
- **Rule 22 — Centered loading spinner and error message.** Centers the loading spinner on every page; on a card's bidding page, it also rewrites and centers the "Auction not found" message.
- **Rule 23 — Custom favicon.** Replaces the site's browser-tab icon with the Wiki-MasterBetter logo.
- **Feature FIS — Fix a page-transition visual glitch.** Corrects a styling issue with the loading spinner that shows up briefly during page transitions (on `/pulls`, `/friends`, `/guild`, `/achievements` and `/profile`).
- **Feature TUT — Built-in feature tutorials.** Adds a "❓" button next to the settings button that opens a modal with short explanations for the script's less obvious features. The modal eases in like the settings tabs, shows the entries in two columns (one on a narrow screen), and starts with a search field (focused on opening) that filters the entries live on their title and text, ignoring accents and case, with a message when nothing matches. An entry that relies on data kept in the browser (sale prices, estimates, followed players, player descriptions — the data Feature BSP exports and imports from your profile) gets a 💾 badge in its top right corner, explained by its tooltip and by a line in the modal's hint.
- **Rule 24 — Bigger, sorted tag list.** Enlarges the tag list on your collection page and sorts it by how many cards carry each tag, from most used to least used. The tag names and amounts are remembered between visits: the remembered amounts (dimmed) show up instantly while the real ones load, then are replaced and the memory refreshed.
- **Rule 25 — Clean up your wishlist.** Adds a button that removes every card you already own from your wishlist, all in one click. The button is always there; when the "wishlist only" toggle is off it shows no count (there is no telling which owned cards are wishlisted), and a card whose modal offers "Ajouter à la liste de souhaits" is just closed, never added.
- **Feature CNC — Copy a card's name.** Adds a small clipboard-copy button next to a card's name wherever that name appears: in the card modal, the auction modal, and a card's bidding page.
- **Rule 26 — Two-column trade lists.** Displays the trades of each tab of `/trades` ("Reçues", "Envoyées" and "Historique") as two flexible columns instead of one long single list, so more of it fits on screen, as soon as the tab shows its trades. A tab with no trade (the "no offer" message) keeps its normal full-width look. The lists are pseudo-virtualized, like the notifications: only the first 20 trades are shown, then 20 more each time you scroll near the bottom of the list (the partner filter of Rule 27 counts the trades it lets through, and starts over from 20 when you change it).
- **Rule 27 — Filter trade history by partner.** Adds a search field above your trade history to filter it down to trades with one specific person, or containing one specific card: the text is matched against the partner's name and against the name of every card of the trade (case and accents ignored, part of a name is enough).
- **Rule 28 — Clear button in the collection search field.** Adds a small "✕" button inside the collection search field to instantly empty it.
- **Feature OWL — Jump straight to a pulled card's Wikipedia page.** While opening packs on `/pulls`, pressing the W key opens the revealed card's Wikipedia page directly, without you needing to first open the card's modal and find the link yourself. (Feature AKP's own on-screen legend documents the shortcut.)
- **Rule 29 — Automatic page reloads when stuck.** On `/pulls`, once your pack pile has reached the maximum size, the page reloads every 10 minutes for as long as it stays full — opening packs cancels the timer instead of letting it reload mid-session. On a card's bidding page, if Rule 22's "Auction not found" message is showing, the page reloads itself once automatically, shortly after. On `/collection`, the page likewise reloads itself once when the site shows a red "a échoué" (failed) message. On any page, it also reloads itself once when an h1 reading "This page couldn't load" appears; since that one could loop on a page that stays broken, it gives up after 3 reloads within a minute (and logs an error) until that minute has passed.
- **Rule 30 — Bigger, reorganized bulk-tagging modal.** Widens the "apply a tag" / "remove a tag" modal on your collection page and lays its tag list out as a wrapping grid instead of one narrow column, so more tags fit on screen at once. On the removal variant specifically, the list is also sorted so the tags affecting the most of your selected cards appear first.
- **Rule 31 — Filter notifications by type.** Adds toggle buttons to the notifications panel's header — one each for sold cards, bought cards, bidding-related notifications (outbid refunds and unsold "returned to you" cards), and wishlist listings — that show only notifications of that type and hide the rest. Only one filter can be active at a time; pressing the active button again clears it. The chosen filter is remembered between visits. When a filter is active and no notification of that type is in the list, a short message ("Aucune notification « {type} » pour le moment.") is shown under the header instead of an empty panel.
- **Rule 32 — Red glow on an almost-finished auction.** On a card's bidding page, the "Se termine dans…" countdown glows red, the same pulsing effect Feature BSP gives a legendary price (in gold), once 15 seconds or fewer remain (configurable in the Config tab: 5, 10, 15 by default, or 30 seconds; the glow follows a change on the next second).
- **Rule 33 — Warn when you're already the highest bidder.** On a card's bidding page, the "place a bid" button turns light red when the current highest bidder is you, as a reminder you don't need to bid again.
- **Rule 34 — Keyboard navigation in the tag filter list.** Opening the tag filter popover on your collection page now moves focus straight into the list (onto the currently selected tag, or the first one), so arrow keys navigate it immediately instead of scrolling the page. Home/End jump to the first/last tag, and typing a few letters jumps to the next matching tag by name (ignoring its leading "#" and usage count).
- **Rule 35 — Your biggest card estimates.** Adds a "Top 40" button (the number follows the "Taille des classements « Top »" setting of Feature CFG, 40 by default) right after the page heading on `/collection` that opens a modal listing the 40 highest card estimates (same configurable size) saved in your cache (Feature ETS), most valuable first, in three columns. Each entry starts with its rank in the list, dimmed, and ends — after the price — with a "✕" button that deletes that estimate from the cache and grays the entry out, just like the cache entries on `/profile`. The same layout is used by the best sales/purchases modals of Feature BBS. The modal fades in smoothly, and the single highest estimate is showcased above the grid with a golden pulsating glow. Cards whose estimate is still unknown ("?") are left out. The last result is buffered in your browser (cache key `wm-top-evals-cache`, not part of the export): opening the modal draws it immediately (the buffer always holds the 100 best estimates and is cut to the configured size when drawn), while the list is recomputed in the background (a small "Actualisation…" shows in the header) and swapped in when finished, so the modal opens fast even with a very large cache. "Vider le cache" removes the buffer too.
- **Rule 36 — Jump to any collection page.** On `/collection`, once the catalog has finished loading, the "Page X / Y" indicator becomes clickable: clicking it swaps it for a number field (1 to the last page). Press Enter to go to that page — the script presses the site's own previous/next buttons for you, one page at a time — or Escape (or click elsewhere) to cancel.
- **Feature BBS — Remember your biggest sales and purchases.** When you open the page of one of your own auctions that has sold, or one that you won, the trade (card name and final price) is saved in your browser, under the cache keys `wm-observed-best-sells` and `wm-observed-best-buys` respectively. Only your 100 biggest of each are kept, sorted from biggest to smallest; the profile modals show as many of them as the "Taille des classements « Top »" setting of the Config tab (40 by default, the same size as the "Top" estimates list), so changing the setting deletes nothing; once full, a new trade only gets in if it beats the smallest one saved. Each price is saved with a short identifier taken from the auction's address (like Feature BSP does), so revisiting the same auction never records it twice. Each trade also stores its cutoff from the card's estimated market value (saved by Feature ETS): how far the price sits above or below it, in whole percent. It is shown as a badge such as "+223%" or "-21%" on the top edge of the trade in the profile modal (hover it for an explanation); trades whose card has no known estimate have no badge. Trades are listed by price by default; a "Trier par bonne affaire" switch in each modal (off until you turn it on, and remembered afterwards) re-orders them by how good a deal they were. The score weighs how far the price sits on the right side of the card's estimate (the premium for a sale, the discount for a purchase) by the logarithm of that estimate, to the 4th power (edge × ln(1 + estimate)⁴), so a 971 sold at +175% scores well above a 250 sold at +216%, and a 605 bought at -64% well above a 13 bought at -93% (trades without an estimate last). Each entry in those modals also exposes its score as a `data-score` attribute (rounded to 2 decimals; absent when the card has no estimate). Already-saved trades get their cutoff filled in or updated automatically. The auctions listed in the "Historique" tab (sales) and the "Gagnées" tab (purchases) of `/marketplace` are picked up the same way, so you don't have to open each one yourself. On your own profile, "Mes plus grosses ventes" and "Mes plus gros achats" buttons between "Ma Collection" and "Mes Amis" open a modal listing those trades, in the same layout as the "Top" estimates (biggest one showcased with a golden glow, "✕" to delete a trade from the cache).
- **Feature FOL — Follow players.** On another player's profile, an eye button next to the "Signaler" button, at the top right of the profile card, lets you follow them (it reads "Suivre", then turns red and reads "Suivi" while they are followed; click again to unfollow). The list of followed players is kept in your browser, under the cache key `wm-followed-users`. On a card's bidding page, a small red eye is then shown at the end of the name of every followed player: the seller, the bidders and the current highest bidder, always on the same line as the name (it never wraps onto a line of its own). On your own profile, the players you follow are listed under the sold-prices block, alphabetically, each linking to their profile, with a "✕" to unfollow them. That list is part of the sold-prices block's export/import (see Feature BSP).
- **Rule 37 — Hide the "favorite" bulk buttons.** On `/collection`, the bulk-action bar that appears at the bottom of the page when you select cards no longer shows its "add to favorites" and "remove from favorites" buttons, leaving only the tagging and discard actions.
- **Rule 38 — Highlight cards without an estimate.** On `/collection`, adds a "Sans estimation" toggle right after the "Top 40" button. While on, every card of the page that has no saved estimate (Feature ETS) gets a light red halo; a card whose estimate is "?" counts as estimated and is left alone. The toggle state is remembered between visits, under the cache key `wm-no-eval-halo`.
- **Feature UDS — Personal descriptions for other players.** On another player's profile, a "Description" button (pencil icon) is added at the top right, right before the "Suivre" button. It opens a small modal with a text field where you can note what that player is after, what they offer, etc.: fewer than 128 characters, with a live counter, on a single line. Press Enter or "Enregistrer" to save (saving an empty text deletes the description); Escape, a click outside or the "✕" close the modal. The description is kept in your browser under the cache key `desc-{username}`, and is displayed, muted and in full, right after the player's name on their profile and on `/friends` (wrapping below the name when there is no room beside it), and under their name in the followed players list of your own profile (Feature FOL). Descriptions are part of the sold-prices block's export/import (see Feature BSP).
- **Rule 39 — Progress bars pinned in the achievement cards.** On `/achievements`, the script looks every 10 seconds for the achievements' progress bars (they appear once the list has loaded). As soon as it finds some, it moves all of them to the bottom right of their card, at half the card's width, without extra margins.
- **Feature TBD — Tag cards during the bulk evaluation.** Off by default (enable it in the ⚙️ settings, then reload). Complements Feature EBC: during a bulk evaluation (page or whole collection), every card whose estimate ends up as "?" gets the TBD tag: "TBD" by default, or any of your own tags chosen in the "Étiquette « TBD »" setting of the Configuration tab (Feature CFG; the drop-down lists the tag names Rule 41 remembered). Cards that already have any tag are left untouched: a value tag is never removed to make room for TBD. Once the market value has been looked up, the script closes the market modal, types the tag name in the card's tag field, presses Enter, and waits until the tag shows among the card's active tags before moving on to the next card. The same feature can also tag a card by its new estimate: in the "Étiquettes automatiques selon l'estimation" setting of the Configuration tab (Feature CFG) you build a list of value ranges, each with a lower bound (value ≥), an upper bound (value ≤) and a tag. When a card's bulk evaluation ends on a number, it gets the tag of every range that contains that number. Each range has its own on/off switch (an off range is kept but not applied). Ranges may overlap, may share the same tag (then applied once) and a blank bound means no bound; a range whose lower bound is above its upper bound matches nothing (it is outlined in red) and a range without a tag is ignored. Unlike the "TBD" tag, these are added to the tags the card already has: a tag it already carries is skipped, and nothing is ever removed. The list is empty by default, so nothing changes until you add a range.
- **Feature MPA — Price arrows on the marketplace.** On `/marketplace`, in the "Parcourir" and "Mes enchères" tabs, every listed card you already have sale prices for (Feature BSP) gets a colored arrow next to its price. The price compared is the listed amount, plus 10% (what a new bid would cost) on a card in the "Surenchéri" state (you were outbid) only, and it is graded like on the auction page (same thresholds, same reference average): gold for a legendary price, cyan extraordinary, purple excellent, green pleasant, amber tolerable, red excessive. A down arrow (▼) means the price is below the reference average (legendary to pleasant), an up arrow (▲) that it is at or above it (tolerable, excessive). Hovering the arrow shows the grade and the reference average; with 2 stored prices or fewer, the arrow is dimmed as an unreliable indication. Cards you have no history for get no arrow, and the arrows disappear when you leave those tabs.
- **Feature LOG — Structured console logging.** WMB now has an internal logger, built on the same pattern as BC-Plume's: every message goes to the matching browser console method (debug, info, log, warn, error) and is prefixed with the script's version, a fixed-width level tag and a millisecond timestamp, e.g. `[WMB_0.26.8 INFO. | 14:02:11.042]`. At startup it logs the initialization, each feature switched off in the settings (debug level) and the end of the initialization; a feature that throws while starting is now reported as an error and no longer prevents the following ones from starting. The version shown is read from the user script manager, not duplicated in the code. Levels: ERROR for something broken (failing feature, failed storage write, unreadable import file); WARN for a recoverable anomaly (corrupted stored data reset, missing page element, retried bulk step, auto-reload caused by a site failure, refused import); INFO for deliberate state changes (settings saved, follow/unfollow, description saved, cache export/import/clear, notifications sent, bulk-run start and end, reloads); LOG for routine traces (a price, estimate or best trade recorded, a toggle flipped, a per-card step of a bulk run); DEBUG for startup detail. Every rule/feature is also traced automatically: the observers and timers each one creates are attributed to it and timed, and the DOM changes they make are counted, which gives one LOG line the first time a rule/feature actually changes the page on a given path (e.g. `[rule-4] observer changed the DOM: attr class on div.friend-list-grid`), plus a LOG digest of every rule/feature that ran (runs, DOM changes, time spent) and an INFO "Navigation" line whenever the path changes. Key calculations (price grades and references, best-trade cutoffs, claim totals, marketplace arrows, estimates shown on collection tags) are logged once per page. A callback that throws is reported as an error instead of an uncaught exception. Hand-written logs stay out of the page-watching loops, which fire constantly.
- **Feature CFG — "Configuration" tab in the settings.** The ⚙️ settings modal is split in two tabs: "Configuration" (shown first), which holds business values and your pseudo, and "Fonctionnalités", which keeps the on/off switches of every rule/feature, unchanged. Each value is chosen with a horizontal pill selector (one pill per possible value, the chosen one highlighted, the first and last slightly wider to make room for the rounded ends; a circled "i" next to each setting's name shows a short explanation of it in a tooltip when hovered, focused or clicked), or with a slider (with its current figure shown beside it) when the value is a number in a continuous range, or with a drop-down when it is one of your own names (a styled list with the current choice ticked, usable with the keyboard; the tag fields of the value ranges suggest your tags the same way as you type) and is saved to your browser (cache key `wm-config`) the moment you click it. Whatever uses a value reads it when it acts, so a change applies without reloading the page (a label or tag already drawn may only refresh on the next page change). A stored value that is no longer a valid option falls back to its default, and every default is the value the script used before it became configurable. Settings are bundled in titled groups (the panel scrolls when it gets tall; two columns, one on narrow screens). Switching tab is animated: the modal eases from its previous width and height to the new ones while the incoming tab fades in (nothing moves if you prefer reduced motion).
  - **Compte** — "Votre pseudo WikiMasters" (read-only text, empty at first): your pseudo, which the sales/purchases features use to recognise your own auctions, sales and bids (grading your sales, Feature BBS, the "already leading" button color, automatic watching when you bid). The field is read-only, with a "✕" next to it that empties it (it is then detected again the next time the profile is read); it is filled in automatically the first time you open your own profile (`/profile`), and a pseudo already detected is never overwritten by a later reading (a different one is only reported as a warning in the log). Until it is known, no auction is considered yours.
  - **Enchères & marché** — "Durée par défaut d'enchère" (10 min, 30 min, **1 h**, 3 h, 6 h, 12 h): the duration Feature DAD selects when the sell modal opens. "Suppression automatique des enchères surveillées" (1 jour, 3 jours, **7 jours**, 30 jours, 1 an): how long after an auction's end watched auctions are removed when you reach your profile (Feature WAT). "Onglet du marché ouvert par défaut" (Parcourir, **Mes enchères**, Historique, Gagnées, Aucun): the tab Rule 9 opens on arrival on the marketplace; "Aucun" opens none, and changing the setting clicks nothing on a page that is already open.
  - **Prix de vente observés** — "Prix de vente gardés par carte" (5, **7**, 10, 15): how many sale prices Feature BSP keeps per card; lowering it deletes nothing at once, a card is trimmed the next time a price is recorded or imported. "Prix retenus pour la moyenne de référence" (3, **5**, 7): how many of a card's cheapest stored prices average into its reference price; it can never exceed the stored-prices setting (it is capped when read, with a warning in the log).
  - **Estimations & classements** — "Taille des classements « Top »" (10, 20, **40**, 60, 100): how many entries the Top estimates list (Rule 35) and the best sales/purchases lists (Feature BBS) show. The lists themselves are always kept at 100 entries (the setting's largest value): the Top estimates buffer and your best sales/purchases are stored at that size whatever the setting says, so lowering it deletes nothing and raising it back shows the entries again at once. "Seuil « sans valeur » des estimations" (a slider from 1 to 100, **10** by default, saved when you let go of it): under this estimate a card's tag shows 💩 instead of the value (Feature ETS).
  - **Estimation en masse** — "Étiquettes automatiques selon l'estimation" (a list you extend with "+ Ajouter une plage": each line is a switch, ≥ lower bound, ≤ upper bound, tag, with a ✕ to delete it — the switch turns a range off without deleting it (it is dimmed and not applied) and back on; up to 30 ranges, empty by default; the tag field suggests your remembered tags but accepts any name; saved shortly after each edit): the value ranges Feature TBD uses to tag cards from their estimate. A card is only touched if it has no tag yet or carries one of the tags defined here (or the TBD tag): the tags of this list, and the TBD tag, that no longer match its new estimate are removed and the matching ones added (an estimate that falls in no range just removes the outdated one); cards carrying only other tags are left alone. "Étiquette « TBD »" (a drop-down, **TBD** by default): the tag Feature TBD puts on cards left without an estimate; it lists your own tags as remembered by Rule 41, always offers "TBD", and keeps the current choice even if it is no longer in the list. "Essais par carte" (1, **3**, 5): attempts a card gets before the bulk evaluation skips it for lack of an estimate (Feature EBC). "Délais d'attente de l'estimation" (Rapide 4 s / 10 s, **Standard 8 s / 20 s**, Patient 15 s / 40 s): how long the bulk evaluation waits for a card's estimate on the first attempt / on the retries. Both are read at the start of each run.
  - **Notifications & alertes** — "Seuil d'alerte de la pile de paquets" (50 %, 70 %, **90 %**, 100 %): the share of the maximum pile (10 packs) from which the pack notification is sent (Feature PNP); the value used is 1 point below the share (90 % uses 0.89) to absorb floating-point error. "Seuil de la lueur rouge de fin d'enchère" (5 s, 10 s, **15 s**, 30 s): the time left at or below which the bid page's countdown glows red (Rule 32).
- **Feature DAD — Default auction duration.** When the card sell modal opens, the duration button matching the "Durée par défaut d'enchère" chosen in the Configuration tab (10 min, 30 min, 1 h, 3 h, 6 h or 12 h; 1 h by default) is clicked for you. It acts once each time the modal opens, can be switched off in the "Fonctionnalités" tab, and logs a warning if the site doesn't offer the chosen duration.
- **Feature WAT — Watch auctions.** Keeps auctions aside so they can be found again from the marketplace. Can be switched off in the "Fonctionnalités" tab.
  - **Watching one:** on an auction page, a "📌 Surveiller" button right after the back button watches that auction (it then reads "📌 Surveillée"; clicking it again stops watching it). On `/marketplace`'s list page, every card also gets a small pin, docked on the top right corner of the card's wrapper (straddling its edge, off the artwork; not shown on the "Gagnées" and "Historique" tabs, whose auctions are over), that does the same (filled while watched) without opening the card. Bidding on an auction also watches it automatically: once the page is loaded and the auction still running, it is watched as soon as you are its current leader or your pseudo appears in its bids history. That is one-way and checked once per auction per page load, so un-watching it by hand sticks until the next reload. Once an auction is certified over (its page has finished loading and no countdown is shown), the button is disabled ("Enchère terminée : rien à surveiller"), unless the auction is still watched (then it can still be un-watched, and is anyway un-watched automatically); it is enabled back if the countdown reappears.
  - **The list:** on `/marketplace`'s list page, a "📌 Surveillées (n)" button next to the page heading opens a popover. Running auctions come first (soonest end first), then those whose end is unknown, then the finished ones; each row is a link to its page with when it ends (e.g. "dans 2 h 5 min", "terminée") and a "✕" to stop watching it. A centered "Ouvrir les enchères terminées (n)" button at the top opens every watched auction whose end is known and already past, all at once and in background tabs (disabled when there are none); each one is then un-watched by the automatic un-watching below.
  - **Storage:** each watched auction is kept in your browser as `watched-{auction id}-{end epoch in ms}-{card name}`, in the array under the cache key `wm-saved`. The epoch is the auction's end, estimated from its "Se termine dans…" countdown, or `???` while unknown (e.g. a pin on a list card that shows no countdown). It is recorded or corrected (drift above 2 minutes, e.g. a last-seconds bid) whenever you display the running auction. Entries saved in the former format (the watch moment in that slot) are told apart by a fixed reference date and rewritten at startup with `???` as their end, keeping their id and name, never deleted.
  - **Automatic clean-up:** visiting a watched auction that is over (its "time left" countdown is absent once the page has finished loading) un-watches it. Every time you reach your own profile (`/profile`), watched entries whose auction ended more than the delay chosen in the Configuration tab ago (1 day, 3 days, 7 days by default, 30 days or 1 year), and unreadable ones, are removed; `???` entries are kept and anything else in `wm-saved` is left alone.
- **Rule 40 — Dim the cards whose arrow points up.** On `/marketplace`, on the "Parcourir" tab only, a "▲ Estomper" switch is added in a dock at the far end of the row of rarity filter buttons (pushed to the right, and kept last even when the site adds its "Réinitialiser" button after picking a filter), styled after them (like the action buttons Feature EBC adds next to the rarity filter on `/collection`). While it is on, the cards whose price arrow (Feature MPA) points up — the price is at or above the reference average, so not a bargain — are dimmed (to 20% opacity by default, configurable); cards with a ▼ arrow, or without an arrow, stay as they are. The switch disappears, and the dimming stops, on the other tabs. The choice is remembered in your browser under the cache key `wm-dim-up-cards`. Can be switched off in the "Fonctionnalités" tab.
- **Rule 41 — Remember the names of your tags.** On `/collection`, whenever the tag filter's list is open, the names of your tags (without their leading "#" and their count) are read and stored in your browser, under the cache key `wm-tags` (not part of the export; opening the list again rebuilds it). The list shows all of your tags, so what is stored replaces what was there before: a deleted tag disappears. It feeds the "Étiquette « TBD »" setting of Feature CFG, which lets you pick which of your tags Feature TBD uses. Can be switched off in the "Fonctionnalités" tab.
- **Rule 42 — Backspace selects the card's tags.** In the owned card modal (`/collection`, `/pulls`), hitting Backspace in the tag input while it is empty moves the focus to the remove button of the card's last tag (nothing is removed by the key itself, and a held key is ignored). Once that button is activated (click, Enter or Space), the focus moves to the previous tag's button, or back to the tag input when no tag is left. While a tag's button has the focus, the Left and Right arrows move it to the previous and next tag's button (stopping at both ends), and Home and End to the first and last one. Can be switched off in the "Fonctionnalités" tab.
- **Rule 43 — Hide the cards outside your wishlist.** On `/marketplace`, on the "Parcourir" tab, a "Cacher indésirables" switch is added right before the "▲ Estomper" one (Rule 40's dock, so Rule 40 must be enabled). While it is on, the cards whose name is not in your wishlist (the "unwanted" ones) are hidden; nothing is hidden while no name is stored. The names are read on `/global-collection`: with its "wishlist only" toggle on, a "Feuilleter (WMB)" button appears next to it (its label shows the progress while it runs). Clicking it makes the script wait for the page to settle (right after a load the site briefly shows the whole catalog, which is ignored), read the names of the cards of the page, then walk the other pages with the site's own "Suivant"/"Précédent" buttons until every page of the wishlist has been read, and goes back to the page you were on. Only a complete walk replaces the names stored in your browser, under the cache key `wm-wishlist-names` (not part of the export), so a card you removed from the wishlist disappears. Click "Feuilleter (WMB)" on `/global-collection` with the toggle on first (nothing is read automatically); the walk is abandoned if the toggle is switched off or the wishlist looks too big to be one. The switch's tooltip shows how many names are stored and how many cards of the page match. The switch state is remembered under the cache key `wm-hide-wished-cards`, and the switch disappears, and the hiding stops, on the other tabs. Can be switched off in the "Fonctionnalités" tab.
- **Rule 44 — Total of the received cards' estimates in a trade.** On `/trades`, when the trade detail modal is open, a dark green "(total {value})" is added at the end of its "En échange de" heading: the sum of the saved estimates (Feature ETS) of the cards listed under it, rounded and spaced by thousands. If some of those cards have no known estimate (missing or "?"), the sum of the others is followed by "+?" ("{value}+?"), or the total reads "?" when none is known. The same estimates are also shown next to the rarity of each card in that modal, as well as in the trade preparation modal ("Contre-offre avec …") wherever it opens (Feature ETS). Can be switched off in the "Fonctionnalités" tab.
- **Feature BSP (cont'd) — Filter the profile's cache entries.** On `/profile`, in the sold-prices block (Feature BSP), a text field right above the entries lets you filter them by card name (case- and accent-insensitive, on part of the name). Entries that don't match are hidden, and all come back when the field is emptied. The field is folded away with the entries when the block is folded. It is part of Feature BSP and has no switch of its own.
- **Feature EXC — Leave some pages alone.** WMB does nothing on `/battle` and `/settings` (it used to be excluded from them in the script header, which can't work on a site that changes page without reloading). If the page is opened on one of them, the initialization waits until you reach another page (only the navbar parts start). Moving there afterwards switches every style WMB added off, and its observers and timers stop acting (the navbar — its pin, ⚙️ settings and ❓ tutorials buttons — is the exception: it stays the same there); coming back to any other page switches everything back on. Page elements WMB had already modified on a previous page may keep their changes until the page reloads.
- **Rule 43 (cont'd) — Keep the wishlist names in sync.** On `/global-collection`, clicking the wishlist button of a card's modal ("Ajouter à la liste de souhaits" / "Retirer…") adds the card's name to, or removes it from, the names stored under `wm-wishlist-names`, without a new walk. Nothing is written while no names are stored yet: only a complete "Feuilleter (WMB)" walk creates the list. Part of Rule 43, with no switch of its own.
- **Feature CFG (cont'd) — Search the features.** At the top of the "Fonctionnalités" tab, a search field filters the switches by identifier or label (case- and accent-insensitive); a message shows when none matches. The modal eases to its new size as the list shrinks or grows (same transition as the tab change). While a search is active, "Tout sélectionner" / "Tout désélectionner" only acts on the switches still shown. Part of Feature CFG, with no switch of its own.
- **Rule 6 (cont'd) — "WMB is running" mark.** Below the site logo, a green "✓" followed by "WMB v{version}" (the version of the installed script, read from the script manager) shows that Wiki-MasterBetter is running; its tooltip spells it out. While the navigation bar is compact only "✓ WMB" is shown (the logo, unreadable once squashed, is dropped in its favour), the full "augmenté par WMB v{version}" is shown once the bar is expanded. Clicking the mark does nothing (it sits inside the logo's link, whose navigation it blocks). Part of Rule 6, switched off with it.
- **Feature UDS (cont'd) — Descriptions in the messaging panel.** When the messaging panel is open (on any page), the personal description of the conversation partner (Feature UDS) is also shown, muted, right after their username in the panel's header, on one line and cut with an ellipsis when too long (hover for the full text). It follows the description as soon as it is saved. Part of Feature UDS, with no switch of its own.
- **Rule 40 (cont'd) — Configurable dimming.** The "Opacité des cartes estompées (%)" setting (Feature CFG, "Enchères & marché" group, a slider from 0 to 80 % in steps of 5, 20 % by default) sets the opacity the dimmed cards keep when "▲ Estomper" is on; the switch's tooltip shows it. It applies without reloading the page.
- **Rule 45 — No spacing between stacked blocks on the collection.** On `/collection`, the vertical spacing the site puts between the children of every `div.space-y-3` (a margin) is nullified, so those blocks sit flush against each other; `div.space-y-3.animate-fade-in-up` is then laid out as a flex column with a 0.5rem gap instead. It follows the page without reloading, and the spacing comes back as soon as you leave `/collection`. Can be switched off in the "Fonctionnalités" tab.
- **Feature EBC (cont'd) — Run statistics.** When the "Estimer tout" run ends, whether it completed or was stopped, a modal sums it up: its duration, the pages walked, the cards targeted, estimated and left unknown ("?"), the cards skipped (listed by name), the retried attempts, the cards tagged (when Feature TBD is on) and, for the estimated ones, their total and average value and the priciest card. Closed by its cross, a click outside or Escape. Part of Feature EBC, with no switch of its own.

- **Rule 46 — Back to your packs after launching an auction.** When you put a card up for auction from `/pulls` ("Lancer l'enchère", by click or with Enter) and the site redirects you to the auction's page, you are sent straight back to `/pulls` so you can keep opening packs. Launching an auction from anywhere else is left alone.

- **Rule 47 — Previous estimate in the auction modal.** In the "Mettre aux enchères" modal, when the card already has a saved estimate (Feature ETS; "?" doesn't count), it is shown as "Ancienne estimation : {value}" in the top right corner of the card summary (formatted like the rarity tags: 💩 under the threshold, "K" above 1000). The value shown is the one saved *before* the modal opened: Feature ETS then replaces it with the modal's current "Moyenne", so comparing the two tells you how the estimate moved. Can be switched off in the "Fonctionnalités" tab.

- **Rule 48 — Bigger profile picture with a size toggle.** On your own profile (`/profile`), the profile picture is enlarged from the site's 3 rem to a square of 8 % of the window width. A small round pill docked to the picture's top border, centered, switches between "↔" (horizontal, the default: 8vw × 8vw) and "↕" (vertical: 8vh × 8vh). The choice is remembered between visits (cache key `wm-avatar-vertical`). Can be switched off in the "Fonctionnalités" tab.
